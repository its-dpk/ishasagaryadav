About Me

My journey, values and commitment to public service

Our Priorities

The issues we are committed to working on

01

Infrastructure & Civic Amenities

We are committed to building modern, well-planned and sustainable infrastructure that improves everyday life for every citizen. Our focus is on better roads, reliable water supply, proper drainage and sanitation, clean streets, efficient waste management, adequate street lighting, accessible public spaces, and improved connectivity.

02

Public Health & Sanitation

We are committed to ensuring accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare while maintaining a clean and healthy environment for every citizen. Our focus is on strengthening public health services, improving sanitation and cleanliness, ensuring proper waste management, maintaining clean drinking water and hygienic public spaces, and promoting preventive healthcare and health awareness.

03

Community Spaces & Environment

We are committed to creating clean, green, and welcoming community spaces that enhance the quality of life for everyone. Our focus is on developing and maintaining parks, playgrounds, open spaces, and community areas while promoting tree plantation, cleanliness, water conservation, and environmental awareness.
